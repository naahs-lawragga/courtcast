HOW TO

Get started

Share your screen in four steps.

iPhone / iPad senderUse iOS 27 / iPadOS 27 or newer with Safari and screen capture support. Earlier versions are not verified.
  1. 1
    Open the viewer

    Open CourtCast on your Chromebook or computer and choose Watch screen.

  2. 2
    Scan using the Camera app

    Open the regular iPhone Camera, point it at the QR code, and open the link in Safari. Do not use the Control Center Code Scanner: closing that scanner's browser can stop sharing. You can also pair manually using the session code.

  3. 3
    Start sharing

    Tap Start sharing to viewer, approve screen capture, and wait until the viewer displays live video. Give the stream a moment to stabilize.

  4. 4
    Go to your Home Screen

    Swipe up to leave Safari in the background. Do not swipe Safari away from the app switcher. On tested iPhones, sharing can continue across the Home Screen and other apps while Safari stays open. iOS may still suspend capture, and protected content may not appear.

More tips & troubleshooting
  • Same Wi-Fi is recommended but not required. An authorized hotspot connection may work; VPNs and network restrictions can affect connections.
  • iPhone internal audio may not be available to Safari. Enable viewer audio if the sender supplies an audio track.
  • Use fullscreen, Fit / Fill and rotation settings to adjust the view.
  • If the stream stops, return to Safari and check whether screen capture is still running. Use Retry video if needed.
  • Stop sharing when finished. Only share content you are authorized to share.

Network tips
  • Same Wi-Fi is recommended, not required. Some networks isolate devices even when connected to the same Wi-Fi.
  • VPNs may interfere with direct connections. Use an approved network configuration; keep any required school or workplace VPN enabled.
  • An iPhone Personal Hotspot can provide Wi-Fi to a Chromebook if permitted. CourtCast may work over that connection, but this is not guaranteed.
  • Different networks may work, but CourtCast has no TURN relay fallback. Internet access and permitted WebRTC traffic are required.

Wired viewing & privacy

A website cannot act as a generic USB phone-screen receiver. On Mac, a supported iPhone can be viewed separately using QuickTime Player's New Movie Recording feature. Other platforms need compatible native software or hardware. No USB mode is offered here.

Privacy and reliability

Only share content you are authorized to share. Keep the random session code private; anyone with it may request your stream. Public PeerJS signaling and STUN are used; some networks need a TURN relay, which is not included. The website does not record streams.

V9.0Security & privacyCurrent

Security beyond screen sharing

  • Manual viewers must be individually approved by the sender. QR links expire after five minutes and are single-use. Scanning a QR and starting browser capture remains an explicit sender action.
  • New protocol binds media calls to a random authorization token, rejects malformed control messages, limits requests and pending connections, and disconnects viewers when sharing stops.
  • Added website security headers, externalized application JavaScript, tightened script and resource loading, and blocked framing.
  • Added security documentation, repository checks and visible privacy controls. This is hardening, not a guarantee against every attack.
V8.5Smart orientation

Automatic sideways-frame correction

  • Enabled the local visual fallback by default in the main viewer, with a clear opt-out and no frame uploads or recordings.
  • Compares all four orientations using higher-resolution sampled frames, explicit recognition outputs and confidence-based decisions. Strong results can rotate immediately; weaker results require confirmation.
  • Handles fixed portrait-sized streams containing sideways landscape content, and checks for a return to portrait. Resets stale decisions when video is replaced.
  • Improved worker loading, error backoff and visible detection status. Manual rotation remains available when text clues are absent or the browser cannot load the recognition engine.
  • Preserved the Liquid Glass interface and animation system. Additional stream tabs retain metadata-based rotation.

  • Added animated tab insertion, removal and repositioning, with independent stream controls and animated grid chrome. Streaming geometry and video frames are not transformed.
  • Added scroll-triggered reveals throughout the changelog and help sections, animated QR framing and waiting placeholders.
  • All new motion respects the animation toggle, reduced-motion settings and active-stream performance safeguards. Browser-owned native select lists and permission dialogs remain outside website control.
