*School and workplace policies may restrict sharing or viewing. Android browsers generally lack the screen capture API, so a native app would be needed to send. Apple mobile browsers may suspend capture when backgrounded. Protected media may appear black. Audio depends on the browser and source.

Wired viewing & privacy

A website cannot act as a generic USB phone-screen receiver. On Mac, a supported iPhone can be viewed separately using QuickTime Player's New Movie Recording feature. Other platforms need compatible native software or hardware. No USB mode is offered here.

Privacy and reliability

Only share content you are authorized to share. Keep the random session code private; anyone with it may request your stream. Public PeerJS signaling and STUN are used; some networks need a TURN relay, which is not included. The website does not record streams.
